# Collective Soulmaking: The Art of Intersubjective Cosmopoeisis


<br/>

![[Screenshot 2026-08-27 at 4.06.12 PM.png]]


"I’m working on a speculative hypothesis right now that any field that is truly sourced by a source-keeper is a form of “*cosmopoiesis*” -- it transmits a current of eros, a powerful creative life force from the Imaginal realm (a hierophanic field of potential). And this reality field will necessarily distort reality through the density and magnetism of its “middle” -- not unlike the way a black hole will bend and distort space-time.
  
This is fine if it is masterfully sourced and held in a way that is consciously, intentionally, and responsibly coherent. For example, I’ve joked about Participation Mystique* being a kind of “temporary autonomous cult (TAC)”. There is something critical to bringing a metamodern awareness to the fact that any intersubjective field will always be a reality fabrication/distortion field. Fields are bounded, porous containers -- lifeworlds --  with generating functions that attract and afford specific ways of seeing and being. That is why Collective Presencing is powerful as an intersubjective psycho-technology -- because it trains the capacity to bring conscious attunement (and the potential of critical discernment) to what we participate in unconsciously, all the time already. We live within and co-create nested reality fields of nation-states, organizations, cultures, tribes and family systems, of agreed-upon scientific paradigms, myths and metaphors. But what makes a reality field potentially dangerous or entrapping in what James Carse calls a “finite game” is when people lose their capacity to be self-aware that they are inside of a game at all, that they are inside of the “matrix”.

So what happens when more people are able to bring conscious awareness to their co-authorship of these reality-distortion fields, and their innate, latent capacity to source-keep new lifeworlds from the Imaginal, or “from the future”? 

I speculate that the highest potential expression of an intersubjective field that is humming with *alignment and integrity* is one where each actor (or whole) is:

1) harmonized through intra-personal coherence, where each actor is presenced and holds the self-aware and sovereign view of recognizing that they are participating in “a finite game that is constitutive, entangled and interpenetrating with the “infinite game”; and

2) attuned to and acts from the field itself ; where each actor can also adaptively attune to and source from the transversal awareness of the group field and its own agentic and animistic desire;

… and when this happens, it will generate the energy to find the recursive equilibrium and toroidal function of exponential consciousness and creativity — cosmopoiesis and the transversal rupture of numinous wholes that desire to come into being. 

You can hold this pattern of toroidal coherence as a fractal field pattern that can be harmonised across the intersubjective fields/systems/ecologies in the meshwork of relationships between self, other and world. A whole or actor might be a “part” in a family system, a “divine figure” within an archetypal pantheon of plural beings in the cosmos. 

However, it is critical to be in a conscious relationship with not simply the creative potential of Collective Soul-Making, but with all of the risks and pitfalls. 

This Collective Soul-making capacity for Imaginal sourcing -- of what dharma teacher Rob Burbea names cosmopoiesis, as the discovery-creation of new realities -- is powerful, but it also amplifies the unconscious, shadow patterns of the field. Not being able to clearly view and own this power can become harmful -- and when it involves others, irresponsible and dangerous. This comes back to why I find Rob Burbea and Catherine McGee’s soulmaking dharma to be such a trustworthy transmission of wisdom -- its ground and centre is always the return to the subtle attunement of the body and its fluctuating states of expansion and contraction. Imaginal practice is part of an ecology of practices that requires deep and equal capacities in cultivating emptiness and metta as well (to nourish view and compassion). 

The intersubjective field becomes a vessel for alchemical transformation, and creative alchemy feeds on what is unprocessed in the psyches of each person -- the karmic material that is asking for active transmutation -- involving a far more entangled and mysterious metaphysics than we understand. And this process is intensely amplified by the eros and life-force generated in the field. I sense that this was the case for each person individually, but even more for the source-keeper of the field. 

This is why the alignment and coherence of the containment and the vessel is so critical. This containment arises from the capacity to meet and hold intensity (in its immensity and subtlety), and to harmonise and balance the “tensegrity” of active polarities towards the centre of presence. And the source-keeper -- the person who calls this field into being and is responsible for it --  is one of the fractal wholes of that containment."
